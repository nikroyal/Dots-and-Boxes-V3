## 2024-06-27 - Daily Goal Streak

**Learning:** Users often complete daily goals but lack an immediate, visible motivator to return the next day. The streak logic existed (`dailyGoalStreak`) but was hidden, losing its retention value.

**Action:** Surface hidden streak values prominently in the UI. When a user completes a daily action, immediately show the streak and a teaser for tomorrow ("Come back tomorrow to keep it going!") to build a habit loop.
